### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an **AI Systems Architect & Software Engineer** passionate about turning passive software into active, sentient-like companions. My work bridges **Sovereign Local Intelligence** (air-gapped local LLMs, neural speech synthesis, computer vision) with **60 FPS Three.js WebGL visual kinetic interfaces** and **autonomous OS-level self-healing actions**.

* 🚀 **Creator & Lead Architect** of the **Brahma AI Ecosystem** (Brahma Sol, Brahma Echo, Brahma Evo).
* 🧠 **Specialties**: Local Neural Voice (Kokoro-82M), AST-sandboxed Auto-Healing, Multi-modal Vision (MediaPipe / OpenCV), Edge Cloudflare Workers, and Autonomous OS Automation.
* 🎥 **Content Creator**: Documenting bleeding-edge AI engineering on YouTube at [**@Buildonaut-AI**](https://www.youtube.com/@Buildonaut-AI).
* 💬 **Ask me about**: Embodied AI, Local LLM quantization & inference, Three.js kinetic rigging, autonomous agent architectures, or desktop runtime optimization.
